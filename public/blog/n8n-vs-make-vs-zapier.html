n8n vs Make vs Zapier: How to Choose a Workflow Automation Platform

By ARKA · Published November 25, 2026

The short answer: Zapier is usually the quickest way to connect popular apps with simple, linear automations. Make.com offers a visual canvas suited to branching, multi-step scenarios. n8n is the most flexible for technical teams and can be self-hosted for data control. The right choice depends on workflow complexity, volume, who maintains it and where your data needs to live.

What these tools do

All three are workflow automation platforms. A workflow starts with a trigger (a new form submission, a paid invoice, a new row in a spreadsheet) and runs a series of actions in other apps: create a CRM record, send an email, update a database, post a message. They save teams from copying data between tools by hand, and all three now offer ways to add AI steps such as classifying or summarising text.

When to choose each

  • Zapier if your workflows are simple and linear, you rely on popular SaaS apps, and non-technical staff will build and maintain automations themselves.
  • Make.com if workflows branch, loop over lists or transform data, and you want a visual view of the whole scenario.
  • n8n if you need custom code, very high volumes, or self-hosting for cost or data-residency reasons, and someone technical will own it.

It is also common to combine them, or to replace one fragile automation with a small piece of custom code when no platform fits well.

The mistakes that matter more than the tool

  • No error handling. Automations fail silently when an app changes its fields or an API key expires. Add failure alerts.
  • No documentation. Nobody remembers in six months what a workflow does or why. Name steps clearly and keep a short description.
  • Built in the wrong account. Workflows built in a contractor's or employee's personal account disappear when they leave. Build in accounts the business owns.
  • Automating a broken process. Fix the process first, then automate it.

Data and privacy considerations

Automations move customer data between systems, so they are part of your privacy obligations. Know which tools the data passes through, which country each one stores it in, and who has access. For Canadian businesses handling personal information, PIPEDA's principles of limiting collection and safeguarding data apply to automated flows as much as manual ones.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is n8n free?

n8n's source is available and it can be self-hosted, which avoids per-task platform fees, but you then pay for and maintain the hosting. It also offers a paid hosted cloud. Check n8n's current licensing and pricing for your use.

Which is easiest for non-technical teams?

Zapier generally has the gentlest learning curve, followed by Make.com. n8n is the most technical of the three.

Can these tools use AI?

Yes. All three can call language models to classify, summarise or draft text as steps inside a workflow.

Can I switch platforms later?

Yes, but workflows have to be rebuilt, not exported. Documenting what each workflow does makes a later migration much easier.
