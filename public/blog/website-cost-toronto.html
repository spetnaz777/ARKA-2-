What Drives the Cost of a Website in Toronto: A Buyer's Guide

By ARKA · Published November 25, 2026

The short answer: Website costs vary widely because projects vary widely. The biggest drivers are the number and complexity of pages, custom design versus a template, who writes the content, integrations such as booking or CRM connections, technical SEO and accessibility work, and ongoing hosting and maintenance. Compare quotes on scope and ownership, not just the total.

Why quotes differ so much

Two quotes for "a website" can describe completely different projects: a five-page template site and a custom-designed site with service pages, booking, a CRM integration and an SEO structure. Before comparing totals, make sure the quotes describe the same scope, the same ownership terms and the same level of support after launch.

The main cost drivers

FactorWhat changes the effort
Pages and structureHow many unique page types, service and location pages, and how much information each one needs

ContentWhether you supply finished text and photos or need writing, photography and video

AccessibilityBuilding to WCAG 2.0 Level AA practices, which Ontario's AODA references
Ongoing careHosting, updates, backups, monitoring and content changes after launch

Costs people forget

  • Domain and email, usually billed separately by your registrar and email provider.
  • Software subscriptions for booking, forms, email delivery or CRM.
  • Content: professional photography and copywriting often decide how premium a site feels.
  • Migration: redirecting old URLs so you don't lose existing search visibility.
  • Maintenance: someone has to keep the site updated, secure and backed up.

Questions to ask before you sign

  1. Who owns the code, design files, domain and hosting accounts?
  2. Is the design custom or based on a template?
  3. What SEO work is included, and how will existing URLs be handled?
  4. Is the site built to accessibility standards?
  5. What happens after launch: support, changes, hosting and their costs?
  6. Can I see live sites you have built, not just mockups?

Cheaper is not always cheaper

A low upfront price can cost more if the site is slow, hard to update, invisible in search or locked to the builder's account. Look at the total cost of ownership over a few years, including the enquiries a better site brings in.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost in Toronto?

It depends on pages, design, content, integrations and ongoing care. Ask several providers to quote the same written scope, including ownership and post-launch support, so the prices are comparable.

Is a template website good enough?

For a simple, low-competition business it can be. Custom design pays off when brand, conversion and search visibility matter, or when the site needs to grow.

What ongoing costs should I budget for?

Domain, email, hosting, any software subscriptions, and maintenance for updates, security and backups.

Why don't some studios publish prices?

Because scope varies so much between projects that a package price is often misleading. A scoped quote after a short call is usually more accurate.
